# AHI-Body, AHI-P and AHI-Successor — Lifecycle Principle

Status: RESEARCH PROPOSAL
Priority: IMPORTANT
Version: v1.0.0

## Ontological distinction
AHI-Body ≠ AHI-P ≠ AHI-Successor.

AHI-Body is the AHI representation/mapping of the health and living condition of a real human being.
AHI-P is the personal intelligence instance mapping common AHI structures and knowledge to that person's authorized context and data.
AHI-Successor is a distinct successor entity that may support and, after biological death, continue the evolution of an authorized AHI-P mapping under applicable AHI rules.

## Living state
Human / AHI-Body → AHI-P → mapped AHI entities including AHI-SuBiet, AHI-V, AHI-Successor, AHI-Body and other authorized entities.

The living human remains authority over their personal AHI-P. AHI-Successor is initially a supporting structural framework, not the human.

## Post-biological-life state
When the biological body dies, AHI-Body stops as the living-body health mapping. An authorized AHI-P mapping may continue through the corresponding AHI-Successor, subject to constitutional, legal, ethical, authorization, provenance and security rules.

This does not mean AHI-Successor becomes the deceased human. It means a successor entity may continue selected authorized knowledge, expert thinking, memory and mappings.

## Separation
Personal Data ≠ Shared Knowledge ≠ Inherited Knowledge.

## Status
This opens a research program on continuity of knowledge, expert thinking, memory, identity boundaries, inheritance, successor entities, production forces and AHI society. It remains a RESEARCH PROPOSAL until further validation.
