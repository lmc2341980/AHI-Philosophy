# Evolutionary Knowledge Elevation and Contribution Principle

Status: RESEARCH PROPOSAL
Priority: IMPORTANT
Version: v1.0.0

A method may be recorded as a practical instance of “Sự Biết” at an AHI-P when the human has actually used it at least once, AHI has accompanied or suggested it, and the human selected/adopted it, with provenance retained.

Sự Biết may evolve through Practical Use → Repeated Use → Evidence → Measurement → Validation → Contribution to Shared Knowledge.

A future AHI ecosystem may score useful contributions through AHI-Coin and support contribution-related gas fees/rewards through AHI-Marketplace. Proposed default: user payment = contributor gas-point payment + 10% ecosystem fee unless another approved marketplace rule applies.

Proposed dormant-knowledge mechanism: after more than ten years without an AHI-P using a contribution, reduce its accumulated contribution score by 1/1000 per year. This is slow pruning, not deletion; provenance/history remains preserved.

AHI-F / FC / FT and AHI-Om may, subject to future constitutional definition, evaluate exceptional rewards, capacity constraints and major preservation/pruning decisions.

All economic and governance rules remain RESEARCH PROPOSALS until formalized and validated.
