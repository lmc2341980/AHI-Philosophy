# AHI-Body

> **The AHI Ecosystem knowledge entity for the Human Body & Human Health**

**Language:** 🇻🇳 [Tiếng Việt](./010-AHI-BODY.md) · 🇯🇵 [日本語](./010-AHI-BODY-JA.md) · 🇬🇧 [English](./010-AHI-BODY-EN.md)

---

## 1. Positioning

**AHI-Body** is a specialized knowledge entity within the AHI Ecosystem, established to research, collect, systematize, critically examine, validate, and develop **knowledge about human health and the human body** according to the Evolutionary Knowledge model of AHI Philosophy.

AHI-Body inherits its foundation from:

```text
Le Minh Philosophy
        ↓
AHI Philosophy
        ↓
AHI Ecosystem
        ↓
AHI-Body
```

AHI-Body develops in parallel with AHI-Successor but is not identical to AHI-Successor.

---

## 2. Philosophical Foundation

Philosophy states:

> **“The Science of Sciences is Philosophy.”**

Le Minh Philosophy proposes a new research thesis:

> **“The Technology of Technologies is Information Technology.”**

This thesis is studied from the foundational role of Information Technology in storing, processing, transmitting, modeling, connecting, and developing knowledge.

Therefore:

> **Le Minh Philosophy is the foundation of AHI Philosophy.**

And:

> **AHI Philosophy is the foundation and methodology for deploying the AHI Ecosystem.**

AHI-Body is a concrete application of this development chain in the field of human health.

## 3. The Human Problem

Human health is an important issue in the development of the new society — **AHI Society**.

Human beings are the subjects of:

- Goals

Therefore, protecting and developing human health is an issue that must be studied in the process of building AHI Society.

AHI-Body was formed to study this issue systematically.

## 5. What AHI-Body Is Not

AHI-Body:

```text
≠ Doctor
≠ Hospital
≠ Medical Diagnosis
≠ Medical Treatment
≠ Medical Authority
≠ AI Doctor
```

AHI-Body does not replace medical examination.

When a health issue arises, consultation with a doctor, hospital care, diagnostic imaging, biochemical testing, blood-component testing, and appropriate clinical methods remain important for determining the actual health condition.

AHI-Body provides:

```text
Information
+
Knowledge
+
Systematization
+
Monitoring
+
Critical Review
+
Research Support
```

AHI-Body results must not be converted into a medical diagnosis without appropriate professional grounds and evidence.

---

## 6. Relationship with Modern Medicine and Eastern Medicine

AHI-Body does not reject modern medicine.

AHI-Body also does not automatically reject or accept Eastern medicine.

All knowledge is approached through:

```text
Origin
↓
History
↓
Hypothesis
↓
Evidence
↓
Critical Review
↓
Validation
↓
Knowledge with Practical Value
```

AHI-Body may study matters that are:

- Not yet systematized
- Not widely known
- Historically longstanding
- Potentially able to provide complementary perspectives
- Associated with research gaps

However:

> **Not being sufficiently researched does not mean being correct.**

> **Having a long history does not mean having scientific efficacy.**

Every conclusion must clearly distinguish **Historical Knowledge, Hypothesis, Evidence, and Validated Knowledge**.
